WHICH IS BETTER?
- Confidence-based: Proactively executes alternate paths when uncertain
  * Achieves 99.3% recovery rate
  * 4x faster recovery on mispredictions
  * Costs: extra power/resources for 87k unnecessary dual-path branches

- Accuracy-based: Reactive, only activates after seeing low accuracy
  * For progressive workloads with improving accuracy: never activates
  * No overhead when accuracy is good
  * Misses opportunities to pre-execute alternate paths

For progressive DAXPY: Confidence-based is better (catches mispredictions proactively)
For stable high-accuracy code: Accuracy-based is better (no unnecessary overhead)
